How it works

A brake light you wear.

A 6-axis motion sensor (IMU) in the control module watches for deceleration. Slow down and the back panel flips from fluoro yellow to bright red. Try it.

Riding normally

Press and hold (or hold Space). Let go and it returns to normal after 2 seconds.

  1. 1

    Ride normally

    LEDs glow bright fluoro yellow so you’re visible from the front, sides and rear.

  2. 2

    Deceleration detected

    The IMU senses deceleration of about 0.3 g or more.

  3. 3

    Brake light on

    The back panel turns bright red, the colour drivers already read as “stopping”.

  4. 4

    Back to normal

    After 2 seconds with no deceleration, the panel returns to yellow.

The red panel responds to deceleration measured by the sensor, not to your brake lever. It supplements, and does not replace, your motorcycle’s own brake light.

Important safety information

Straight talk before you buy.

It’s a visibility aid, not protective gear

LUMO SHELL is designed and sold as a visibility over-jacket. It is not sold as impact or abrasion protection and does not replace a helmet or protective riding gear. Wear it over your normal riding jacket.

Your bike’s brake light still does the real work

The red panel reacts to deceleration measured by a motion sensor, not to your brake lever. It may light when you slow by rolling off the throttle or engine braking, and it will not light for very gentle slowing below the sensor threshold. Treat it as an extra signal.

Check the rules where you ride

Road rules about lights and colours that can be shown on or by a vehicle and rider differ between Australian states and territories. You are responsible for checking they allow use of the jacket’s lighting where you ride.

Water-resistant, not submersible

IPX4 means protection against splashing water, such as rain and road spray. Do not submerge the jacket or electronics. Remove the battery pack and control module before washing and follow the care label.

FAQ

Questions riders ask.

Will it fit over my riding jacket?

Yes. LUMO SHELL is designed to be worn over any riding jacket, with stretch panels, side expansion zippers and adjustable straps. If you are between sizes or wear bulky armour, size up. Contact us if you would like help choosing.

Is it waterproof?

It is water-resistant to IPX4, which covers rain and road spray, with all LED channels sealed. It is not designed to be submerged.

How does the brake light know when I’m slowing?

A 6-axis accelerometer (IMU) in the control module senses deceleration. When it detects roughly 0.3 g or more, the back panel turns bright red. After 2 seconds with no deceleration it returns to normal.

How long does the battery last and how do I charge it?

The standard 7.4 V 8000 mAh pack runs up to 8–10 hours of continuous use and charges over USB-C (5 V / 2 A). The Extended battery upgrade gives longer runtime than the standard pack.

Can I wash it?

Yes. The battery pack and control module detach. Remove them before washing and follow the care label.

What are the upgrades?

Colour-change LEDs, brightness control and an extended battery. Add any one for $749, any two for $999, or choose LUMO SHELL MAX with all three for $1,199.

Is it legal to use on the road?

LUMO SHELL is worn clothing, but rules about lighting and colours differ between states and territories and we cannot give you legal advice. Please check the rules where you ride. See “Important safety information” above.
